Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download Cherry from the App Store and sign up using your email address. You can also sign in with Apple for a faster setup.

How do I save products I like?

Simply tap the heart icon on any product to save it to your liked items. You can also add products to custom collections for better organization.

How do I follow a brand?

Visit any brand's page and tap the "Follow" button. You'll see their products in your feed and stay updated on new arrivals.

How do I purchase a product?

When you're ready to buy, tap on a product and then tap "Shop Now." You'll be taken directly to the brand's website to complete your purchase.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Profile, tap Settings (gear icon), and select "Delete Account." This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.

Is Cherry free to use?

Yes! Cherry is completely free to download and use. We connect you directly to brand websites when you're ready to shop.

How can I report a problem?

Please email us at hello@shopcherry.co with a description of the issue and we'll look into it right away.
